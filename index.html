What you're looking at

A real simulation of Schrödinger's equation, the rule that governs how an electron moves. The glowing surface is the electron's wave: the taller and brighter it is, the more likely the electron is to be found there. The colours show the wave's phase (where it is in its up-down cycle).

  • Silver nozzle = electron gun
  • Brass wall with slits = the barrier
  • Frosted strip = detector screen; each dot = one electron detected

Why it matters: one electron goes through both slits at once and interferes with itself. This is why chips, lasers, MRI and electron microscopes work, and why quantum tunnelling limits how small transistors can get.

Try: press Fire. Slide Energy and watch the stripes get wider or narrower. Switch to Tunnel wall to see part of the electron pass through a wall it can't classically cross. Auto-fire builds the stripe pattern one electron at a time.
